# Style notes: The Charyapada

Standing preferences for this text. They travel with every drafting pack, so
a choice made once reaches every later song. Add to this list as you review.

- Translate the image, not its esoteric referent: the boat stays a boat, the mouse a mouse, Ḍombī stays Ḍombī. The reading goes in the notes, attributed.
- Keep each half-line as its own English line; do not merge or reorder halves.
- Keep the poet's self-naming in the last couplet (the bhaṇitā), e.g. "Lūyī says…".
- Plain contemporary English. No archaisms (thee, thou, lo), no explanatory padding, no brackets inside verse lines.
