## Corrections to outline.md, found while checking the sources

Each item was checked against Chien's PDF text, Garfield, the *Blue Annals* scan, BDRC P1830 and Pearcey's translation.

| Outline says | What the source actually supports |
|---|---|
| Three biographies "all by people who knew him (C p. 12)" | P. 12 doesn't say this. Use pp. 20 (Zhönnu Gyaltsen met him), 39 (Pelgyi Rinchen met him, likely finished 1383), 54 (Palden Yeshe, his disciple) and 202 (the interviews). |
| "Gyalse" = son of the Victorious One (C pp. 72–73) | Pp. 72–73 have "Great Bodhisattva" (*rgyal sras chen po*) and the name explanation. The literal gloss "son of the Conqueror, the Buddha" is **n. 144 on p. 74**. Cite pp. 72–74. |
| Full ordination at Bodong É, about 1325 (C p. 86); age 30 (C p. 68) | Chien contradicts herself: p. 68 says "He received full ordination **there** at age thirty", where "there" is the Sakya monastery; p. 86 says Bodong É, 1325. Handle it in a footnote. |
| Age 32 "(about 1326)" (C pp. 68–69) | Chien gives only the age. The year 1326 comes from BDRC. |
| Footnote: 30 vs 29 is due to Tibetan age counting | No source says this. Leave the explanation out (WP:NOR); just report that the sources differ. |
| "Eight Tibetan commentaries from the 19th–21st centuries" (C p. 155 n. 305) | It's what the TBRC (now BDRC) catalogue listed in 2015, not a total. Say that. |
| *Blue Annals* "usually cited as 1976" | The archive.org scan shows no reprint year, only "First Edition: Calcutta 1949". Confirm 1976 from a catalogue record, or cite the 1949–53 Calcutta edition (same page numbers). |
| *Blue Annals* pp. 584–585 | Confirmed: two abbots of Densa Thil (gDan-sa-thel) "heard" doctrines from him. Note that p. 585 has Roerich's aside calling him "a disciple of Bu-ston"; leave that out, since sources disagree. |
| Tara: "until age 41 (HoC)" | Chien calls the post "manager" (*den sa pa*), not abbot. BDRC's note says "abbot of rta ra", 1326–1333. |

Also confirmed:
- Chien p. 18 gives the date *Guide* records for his death: "October 20th in the Year of the Rooster, 1369". It is probably a Tibetan lunar date, so give the year only.
- Chien p. 1 says Bodong É is about 40 miles **southwest** of Ngulchu, which agrees with p. 67 n. 124 (Ngulchu is 40 miles northeast of Bodong É).
- Chien p. 16 n. 23 confirms that the Palden Yeshe excerpt is on pp. 9–26 of *The Heart of Compassion*.
- BDRC P1830 lists his students: Rendawa (P60), Palden Yeshe (P0RK972), Buddhaśrī (P3296), Nyungne Lama Sönam Zangpo (P2460) and Nugo Gyalwa Rinchen (P2079). It also describes him as an "important bka' gdams master".
- Pearcey's translation has 37 refrains ("The practice of all the bodhisattvas") and four closing verses, the last a dedication.

## Garchen Rinpoche

**Question:** can the article mention his special connection to Tokme Zangpo?

**What was found:**
- **Not found:** Chien, Garfield and the *Blue Annals* never mention him.
- **His biography** (Milarepa Retreat PDF) covers his 20 years in prison and the Dzogchen teachings he received there from Khenpo Münsel. It doesn't mention Tokme Zangpo or the text.
- **No emanation claim:** no source says he is an emanation of Tokme Zangpo. His recognised line is Gar Chödingpa.
- **What is documented:** he promotes the text constantly and calls it "nothing other than my substitute". But this appears only on his own organizations' pages (37practices.info, garchencanada.com). His book *The Path to Inner Peace* is published by his own foundation (Edition Garchen Stiftung). English Wikipedia's "Garchen Rinpoche" article supports "widely promotes" only with his own website.

**Why not add it now:**
- **Due weight:** the only sources are self-published by his organizations. Chien names Dilgo Khyentse, Geshe Jampa Tegchok, the Dalai Lama and the Karmapa, which is why the draft lists those four.
- **Conflict of interest:** your study groups are "Endorsed by Garchen Rinpoche", and the book you promote is by one of his students. Reviewers would likely read the addition as promotion.

**Options:**
1. After acceptance, link the text's mention in the "Garchen Rinpoche" article to the new article (already in README Phase 4).
2. If an independent source turns up (a newspaper, a magazine such as Lion's Roar or Tricycle, or an outside publisher's book), propose one sentence on the article's talk page with `{{Edit COI}}` rather than adding it yourself.
3. If "special connection" means a specific published claim, find where it is published and check it before using it.
