# Sources: verified bibliographic data

Everything below was checked against a library catalogue, DOI registry, publisher listing or the document itself on 26 September 2026. Anything unconfirmed is marked **unverified**.

**How to turn these into citations:** in the Wikipedia editor, click **Cite → Automatic** and paste the DOI, ISBN or URL. The tool fills in the citation template for you. Then compare its output with the data below and correct anything it gets wrong. It often mangles Tibetan names, so check those.

Legend: ★ = cite in the article body; FR = "Further reading" only; EL = "External links" only; ✗ = don't use.

## 3. Tibetan-language primary sources (optional; BDRC work IDs)

## 5. Do NOT use

| ✗ | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ✗ | **37practices.space (your own site)** | Conflict of interest ([WP:COI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Conflict_of_interest), [WP:LINKSPAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Spam#External_link_spamming)). Adding it can get the whole draft treated as promotion. Leave it out completely, including from External links. |
| ✗ | Rigpa Wiki, Encyclopedia of Buddhism, Tsadra wikis, Samye wiki | User-generated wikis ([WP:UGC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources#User-generated_content)). |
| ✗ | German, French or Tibetan Wikipedia | Wikipedia can't cite itself ([WP:CIRCULAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Verifiability#Wikipedia_and_sources_that_mirror_or_use_it)). They also contain errors. |
| ✗ | Facebook groups, blogs, dharma-centre "about" pages, the Sakya Tradition app page | Self-published and unreliable. Several give wrong dates. |
| ✗ | Khenpo Sodargye free PDF | No publisher and no translator named. Fine to read, not to cite. |
| ✗ | Penor Rinpoche, *Path of the Bodhisattva* (Vimala) | Not a commentary on the 37 Practices; it's a prayer book that contains the root text. |
| ✗ | Jinpa, *Mind Training: The Great Collection* (2006) | Contains none of Tokmé Zangpo's works. Useful background on lojong only. |
| ✗ (for now) | *Bodhisattva Awakening* (Wisdom, ISBN 979-8-89070-066-7) | Not published until 27 October 2026. Once it's out, Jinpa's introduction will be a strong source on his life. |
